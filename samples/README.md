# Samples

## wane.wav

Made for Grains. It is
[Wane](https://github.com/mmockett/OP-XY-Hexagon/tree/main/wane), a synth
from OP-XY-Hexagon (also by Max Mockett, MIT), rendered on the host through
its own engine: a held C4 (261.6 Hz) with the octave above it at 0.4, on
Worn-like tape (shape swept 0.28 to 0.72 and back once, detune 0.35, drift
0.3, warmth 0.35, wow 0.25, flutter 0.15, sag 0.3, age 0.15). It is six
seconds of 16-bit mono at 44.1 kHz, peaking at -6 dBFS, with its end
crossfaded into its start so a scan wraps without a seam. No recording or
third-party sound is in it.
